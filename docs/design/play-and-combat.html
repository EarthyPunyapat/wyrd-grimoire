You shoulder the door and the smell of tar and wet wool rolls over you. A dozen fishermen go quiet over their cups. Behind the bar, a broad man with a burn scar down one forearm sets down the tankard he was drying and watches your hands, not your face.

By the hearth, a hooded drinker sits with his back to the wall. His ale hasn't been touched.
