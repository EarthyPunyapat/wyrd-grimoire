# Workstream E — time skips and the god-game TDD plan

Branch: `w2-skips`. Scope: roadmap E, design §10 and D153; reuse D's canon clocks and F's delegation.

1. **Daily simulation (red → green):** compare a 30-day skip with 30 explicit daily `runTicks` advances, including four weekly ticks and identical RNG/replay. Accept a duration or a validated state condition, retain legacy minute inputs, bound conditional skips in tuning, and return a recorded digest. Refuse skips during combat or after death.
2. **Watch state and interruptions (red → green):** replay WatchAdded/WatchRemoved; stop at the first daily boundary containing a watched major event, a canon clock entering its final pending segment, or a blocked task. Cover tasks/canon advancing daily and early/unsatisfied conditions. New events/handlers live only in the skips domain pair.
3. **Reports and observation (red → green):** persist a report card per watched entity per skip, including quiet periods. Give an effective Level-∞ observer access to unknown entities while finite observers remain knowledge-limited. Observation and reports explicitly project safe fields; private NPC minds and secret facts never enter model/browser output.
4. **Tool/API/UI (red → green):** move time_skip and observe registration out of core into `gm/tools/skips.ts`, import once, and add watch controls/tools. Expose a safe watch-list projection, use it in Cockpit's rail, and exercise a real offline GM turn plus desktop/phone browsers.
5. **Finish:** focused red/green runs, `bun run check`, build and sequential browser validation (one worker/browser at a time). Update spec/default decisions/handoff/roadmap, integrate current main, merge to main and push to GitHub, then stop. Owner-only W2 checks remain unticked.

Engine conditions use explicit task/clock/fact predicates rather than model-decided completion. Canon interruptions leave time to intervene before resolution. Duration parsing, simulation bounds, severity ranking and report limits are configurable. Reports reveal public/known information and safe resolved event summaries; they never copy arbitrary NPC state or event payloads.
