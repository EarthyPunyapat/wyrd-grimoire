Privacy

Your campaigns stay on your machine

Wyrd Grimoire is a self hosted application. It is not a service, there is no account, and nothing is sent to whoever maintains the project.

What stays local

  • Each campaign is one SQLite file plus a media folder, under your own home directory.
  • Your passcode, provider keys and OAuth tokens live in a secrets folder that is never logged.
  • Model settings and usage records are stored in your local database.

What leaves, and only when you ask it to

If you connect a model provider, the text of your turn and the context the engine assembles are sent to that provider. That is how narration happens. Which provider receives it depends on the route you configure:

  • ChatGPT plan requests go to OpenAI under your account.
  • DeepSeek, Anthropic or any OpenAI compatible server go to that host.
  • Ollama or a local server keeps everything on your own network.

In every case the provider's terms and retention apply to what you send them. You can run the whole thing offline, with no model calls at all, using the offline preview.

This page

The page you are reading is static. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics, and embeds no tracking pixels. It loads one typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch that file.

Removing your data

Delete the campaign file and the secrets folder. There is no copy anywhere else to clean up.
